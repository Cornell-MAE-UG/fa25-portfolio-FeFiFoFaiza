This project was the design and development of a competition robot built to deploy arms and collect blocks during matches. The robot combined mechanical, electrical, and software systems to complete game tasks efficiently and reliably under competition conditions. The system was designed with a focus on fast arm deployment, secure block collection, and consistent driver control throughout operation.

## My Contribution

My contribution focused on nearly all aspects of the robot’s development, including:

- Programming the robot’s control systems and mechanism automation  
- Creating the CAD models and mechanical layout for the robot and arm systems  
- Completing the electrical wiring and overall system integration  
- Testing, troubleshooting, and refining the robot for competition performance  

## Key Engineering Concepts

- Mechanical design and CAD modeling  
- Robot programming and control systems  
- Electrical wiring and component integration  
- Iterative testing and performance optimization
